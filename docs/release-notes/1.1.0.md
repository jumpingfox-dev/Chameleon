# Chameleon 1.1.0 (build 2)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
Each profile now keeps its own theme, fonts, subtitle and playback settings.
Fresh Material design with a cleaner TV sidebar and more breathing room.
Smoother 4K HEVC on Android TV, and TrueHD audio now plays.
Fixed Back exiting the app after watching on TV.
Loading placeholders on Home, and edit mode keeps your place.
Subtitle height is now a slider.
Back button in the TV player.
```

## Full changes

**New**
- Settings are saved per profile: theme, custom theme, fonts, icon style, playback and
  subtitle settings, and the home layout. Existing choices carry over to every profile.
- Material 3 design throughout, with a see-through TV sidebar that opens over a gradient.
- Placeholder tiles while Home loads, and in edit mode.
- Subtitle height is a 1–20% slider.
- A Back button in the player on TVs.

**Playback**
- 4K HEVC plays smoothly on Android TV: video is decoded and drawn by the hardware
  whenever the device supports the format.
- Surround passthrough only sends formats the TV or receiver accepts; anything else (often
  TrueHD) is decoded on the device instead of failing.
- Converted streams use Dolby Digital Plus for surround instead of copying TrueHD.

**Fixes**
- Back on a TV remote no longer exits the app after closing the player or a settings list.
- Moving a section in edit mode keeps the cursor on it.
- Sidebar links no longer shift when it opens or when the avatar is highlighted, and icons
  sit centered in their highlight.
- Subtitles no longer show a yellow underline.

**Polish**
- More room around page edges on TV, matching the sidebar.
- Bigger sidebar icons, logo and home tiles on TV.
- Rounded text fields and pill-shaped search boxes.
- The open sidebar shows your name by your avatar; the logo's name fades in with the labels.
- The player's back button, logo and title sit further from the top edge.
