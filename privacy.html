Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

The short version: Chameleon doesn't collect anything. No analytics, no accounts on our end, no server of ours in the middle. The app runs on your device and talks directly to the Jellyfin server you point it at.

This policy explains that in a bit more detail, in plain terms.

Data we don't collect

Chameleon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, or analytics to us or to any third party. We don't run a backend and there is no Chameleon server standing between you and your media — the app runs entirely on your device and talks directly to the Jellyfin server you point it at.

What stays on your device

Your server address, login session, and app preferences — theme, layout, playback settings — are stored locally on your device using standard platform storage. None of it is sent to us, because there's no "us" anywhere in that path: just your device and your server.

Your Jellyfin server

Chameleon is a client, not a service. Your library, watch history, user account, and any SyncPlay sessions live on the Jellyfin server you choose to connect to, governed by however that server's administrator — who might be you — has configured it. We have no access to it and no way to see it.

Casting and other platforms

Casting video to a Chromecast, or audio to AirPlay on iOS, uses the device discovery and streaming protocols built into Android and iOS by Google and Apple respectively. We don't add tracking on top of that, and no information about your casting session passes through us.

Third-party services

Chameleon doesn't embed analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising libraries. The only network connections the app makes are ones you've effectively configured yourself: to your Jellyfin server, to a casting device on your own network, and — if you choose a custom theme font — to Google Fonts to load the typeface.

Your data

Since we don't collect or store anything, there's no personal information held by us to manage, export, or delete. What you'd want to manage — library data, watch history, account details — lives on your Jellyfin server, and is managed there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date above. We don't collect contact details, so we have no way to notify you directly — check back here if you want to be sure.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can go through the project's GitHub repository: github.com/jumpingfox-dev/chameleon/issues.
